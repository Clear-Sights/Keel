# DL-03 NOVERDICT [dispatch/break] dispatch._guard_marker / GUARD regex; _open_effect_denial's `if cl.id in committed: progress = True; continue`

## claim
"under an open demand a Bash call passes on a leading `# keel-guard: <clause ids>` line naming what it will pay, and is CHECKED after it ran: a committed call whose effect did not pay is a broken commitment ... and the demand stays open." Combined with _open_effect_denial's "the session cannot proceed past a destructive effect it never looked at".
